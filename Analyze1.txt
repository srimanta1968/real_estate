DealEval - Property Data Extractor

Summary from package

Extract property data from NLS/MLS listing sites and send to DealEval for instant investment analysis.

Description
DealEval helps real estate professionals instantly evaluate investment properties by extracting listing data from major real estate websites.

  SUPPORTED SITES:
  • Zillow - Residential listings
  • Realtor.com - Residential listings
  • Redfin - Residential listings
  • LoopNet - Commercial listings
  • Crexi - Commercial listings
  • Trulia - Residential listings
  • CommercialCafe - Commercial listings

  KEY FEATURES:
  • One-click property data extraction from any supported listing page
  • Multi-site search — search across all 7 sites simultaneously from one dashboard
  • Auto-extract price, beds, baths, sqft, year built, lot size, and more
  • Automatic data forwarding to DealEval web app for analysis
  • No manual data entry — the extension reads the listing page for you

  INVESTMENT METRICS CALCULATED:
  • Cap Rate (Capitalization Rate)
  • Cash-on-Cash Return
  • Internal Rate of Return (IRR)
  • Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)
  • Gross Rent Multiplier (GRM)
  • Monthly & Annual Cash Flow
  • 10-Year Financial Projections
  • Operating Expense Ratio

  HOW IT WORKS:
  1. Install the extension and sign in to your DealEval account
  2. Browse any supported listing site (Zillow, Realtor.com, LoopNet, etc.)
  3. Click the extension icon → "Extract" to pull property data automatically
  4. View instant investment analysis with all key financial metrics on DealEval
  5. Compare multiple properties side-by-side across cities and states
  6. Export professional PDF reports to share with clients, partners, or lenders

  WHO IT'S FOR:
  • Real Estate Investors — evaluate deals in seconds, not hours
  • Agents & Brokers — impress clients with professional investment reports
  • Lenders — assess deal viability with DSCR and cash flow analysis
  • Property Managers — analyze acquisition opportunities quickly

  COMPARISON & REPORTS:
  • Compare 2-10 properties side-by-side with visual charts
  • Cross-market comparison with location context
  • Professional PDF reports with metrics, charts, and recommendations
  • Share comparison links with clients and partners

  PRIVACY & SECURITY:
  • No tracking or analytics — your data stays between your browser and DealEval
  • Property data is only extracted from pages you actively visit
  • No background scraping or data collection
  • See our full privacy policy for details

 FREE TO USE:
  The extension is completely free to install and use for extracting property data.
  PDF report generation and advanced comparison features are available through
  DealEval subscription plans at https://dealeval.projexlight.com/pricing
